Japan's agricultural workforce 
is **rapidly aging**.

Many farmers are already **over 65 years old**, 
creating labour shortages and 
increasing the **need for technologies** 
that improve productivity and accessibility.

---
### Why is Smart Agriculture important in Japan?

> **"One of the biggest challenges is that fewer young people are entering agriculture while the farming population continues to age.**
> 
> Smart agriculture isn't about replacing farmers. It's about helping them work more efficiently and making agriculture **more accessible for future generations**."
