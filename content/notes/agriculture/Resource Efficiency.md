Resource efficiency means 
**producing more food while 
using fewer natural resources**. 

Rather than maximizing production, the goal is 
to **reduce unnecessary** consumption of water, energy, fertilizer, 
and space **without compromising** crop quality.

---

For this objective, [[Precision Agriculture]] is used in [[Controlled Environment Agriculture]] along with [[Resource-Constrained Systems]]. Although this remains to be properly investigated. 

Hence, the resources needed to ensure [[Food Security]] are less, so that [[Long term Thinking]] is in mind for the future and reduce food imports.
