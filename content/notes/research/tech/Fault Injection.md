Fault Injection **deliberately introduces** 
**failures** into a system to *observe* how it behaves.

It is commonly used in [[Reliability Engineering]].

---
## How will you evaluate reliability?

>**"Instead of waiting for failures to happen naturally, I plan to create controlled failures during testing.**
>
>For example, disconnecting a sensor or simulating communication delays allows me to evaluate how well the system can recover and continue operating."
