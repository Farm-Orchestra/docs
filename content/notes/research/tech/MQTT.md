MQTT is one of the most 
widely used communication protocols for IoT.

It follows a **publish/subscribe** model 
and was specifically designed 
for devices with limited resources.

---
### Why MQTT?

> **"MQTT is lightweight and very efficient for small IoT devices.**
> 
> Since many agricultural sensors only send small amounts of data, MQTT allows them to communicate reliably while using very little bandwidth and energy. Since [[Edge Computing]] has some limitation, it fits perfectly for that."
