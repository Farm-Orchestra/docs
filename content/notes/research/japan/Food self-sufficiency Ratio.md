## Global Concern

Global [[Food Security]] is becoming an increasingly urgent concern. Despite differences in national contexts, countries worldwide share the goal of ensuring stable access to food[^1].

That means that we are all on the same page in regards to food production and accessibility. 

## Definition of FSSR

For improving the country's food self-sufficiency ratio, the expansion of *"domestic food production"* is needed. 

> [!info] Definition of FSSR by FAO
> The extent to which a country is able to meet its food needs with its own resources.

Due to the unstable political situation world-wide, the dependence on imported food may be aggravated in the future. 

That is why [[Fostering Public Understanding]] on how to become more self-sufficient, grow your own food and learning about smart agriculture techniques, will definitely improve this ratio. 

## How to calculate 

1. Covering both primary and secondary products 
2. Converting all food items into calories 
3. Calculating from the supply difficulties such as food losses or inventory rather than demand.
