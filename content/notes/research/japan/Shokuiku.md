Shokuiku (食育) means **food education**.

It promotes a **deeper understanding** of food, 
nutrition, agriculture and where our meals come from (See [[itadakimasu]]).

The concept is officially supported by the Japanese government through the [[Basic Act on Food]] on Shokuiku.

---
### Why is food education important?

> **"I think technology should help people reconnect with food, not become more disconnected from it.**
> 
> That's why I really like the idea of _Shokuiku_. It reminds us that food isn't only something we consume—it's something we should understand and value. I think **technology can support that idea** rather than replace it."
