Monozukuri literally means *"making things"*, 
but it also reflects a philosophy of **craftsmanship**, 
**continuous improvement** and **creating with care**.

It values **quality**, **precision** and [[Long term Thinking]] over quick results.

---
### Why Japan culturally?

> **"During my visit to Japan I realized that people often approach their work with a level of care and attention that really resonated with me.**
> 
> Later I learned about the idea of _monozukuri_. I think that mindset fits very well with research, where progress often comes from continuous improvement rather than quick results."
